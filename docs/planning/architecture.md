# Architecture: [project name]

> Only fill this in once the PRD is stable. Keep it as simple as the project
> allows. This is not the place for speculative future-proofing.

## Overview

One or two sentences: what are the moving pieces, at a glance?

## Components

List each piece and its one job. If a component's job needs a paragraph to
explain, it's probably two components.

## Data flow

How does a request/action move through the system, start to finish?

## Tech choices & rationale

For each non-obvious choice: what did you pick, and why that over the
alternative? (Skip anything where the "obvious" choice was obvious.)

## Testing strategy

What gets a test, and at what level (unit vs. integration)? What's
deliberately *not* tested, and why that's an acceptable risk?
